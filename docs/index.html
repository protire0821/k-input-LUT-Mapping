k-LUT Mapper in your browser

Technology mapping for FPGA-style look-up tables. Paste a combinational BLIF netlist, pick a LUT size, and get the mapped LUT network back — the C++17 mapper compiled to WebAssembly, running locally on this page. Nothing is uploaded.

BLIF→AIG→k-feasible cuts→depth mapping→area recovery→LUT network
